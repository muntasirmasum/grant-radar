Purpose

The National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases (NIAID) Office of Research Training and Special Programs (ORTSP) will host an informational webinar to provide a program overview about Research Training and Career Development awards and respond to questions from prospective applicants for Fellowship (F) and Career Development (K) awards that are sponsored by NIAID. The information in this webinar will only be relevant for currently active NOFOs and will not address any proposed or upcoming changes to these awards.
