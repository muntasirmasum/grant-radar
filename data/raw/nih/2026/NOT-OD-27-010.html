Purpose

The National Institutes of Health (NIH) will host an informational webinar offering an overview of the NIH Research Education Programs - R25 (Mentoring Activities & Networks; Courses, Curriculum & Methods; and Research Experiences) and answering questions from prospective investigators. Join staff from the Division of Biomedical Research Workforce (DBRW) and NIH Institutes and Centers (IC) for this live technical assistance session on Thursday, November 19, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. ET. The webinar will provide an overview and guidance on programmatic considerations, the application process, and grant-writing strategies. The webinar will be recorded.
