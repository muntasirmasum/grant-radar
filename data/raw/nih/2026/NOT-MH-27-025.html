Purpose

The National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH) will host a pre-application and technical assistance webinar on December 9, 2026, at 1:00 pm Eastern Time (ET) to provide information and respond to questions from prospective applicants who plan to submit applications in response to RFA-MH-28-145 "Research on System Support and Tools for Provider Training and Quality Monitoring for Suicide Preventive Care (RP1 Clinical Trial Optional)".

NIMH staff members involved in managing RFA-MH-28-145 will provide an overview of the RFA, including objectives, application instructions, and review criteria, and will be available to answer questions.

Participation in this webinar, although encouraged, is optional and is not required for the submission of an application in response to RFA-MH-28-145. A recording of the teleconference will be available after the TA meeting.
