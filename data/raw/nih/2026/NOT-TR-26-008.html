Purpose

This Notice informs potential applicants that the National Center for Advancing Translational Sciences (NCATS) is participating, effective immediately, in the Notice of Funding Opportunity Announcement (NOFO) PA-24-201, "Emergency Competitive Revision to Existing NIH Awards (Emergency Supplement - Clinical Trial Optional)".

The following sections of PA-24-201 have been modified (changes shown in bold italics) to reflect NCATS' participation in this NOFO:
