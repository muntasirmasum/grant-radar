Related Announcements

Purpose

This notice informs prospective applicants that the National Institute of Dental and Craniofacial Research (NIDCR) will host a public pre-application informational webinar for the following two Notices of Funding Opportunities (NOFOs). Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) related to these NOFOs are also available.

  • PAR-27-015, Community-Based Participatory Research to Advance Data and Practice Transformation (ADAPT) for Optimizing Oral Health for All (UG3/UH3 Clinical Trial Optional)
  • RFA-DE-27-001, Community Engagement Evaluation and Data Coordination (CEED) Hub to Advance Data and Practice Transformation for Optimizing Oral Health for All (U01, Clinical Trial Not Allowed)

Webinar Information

Date: Wednesday, June 24, 2026

Time: 2:00 - 3:00 PM Eastern Time

Registration web-page: https://www.nidcr.nih.gov/news-events/events/nidcr-preapplication-informational-webinar-community-based-participatory-research

Pre-registration is required. During the informational webinar, NIH staff will provide an overview of the funding opportunities and answer questions from prospective applicants. Attendees are encouraged to submit questions in advance at least 5 business days before the webinar to [email protected]. Webinar participation is optional and not required for application submission.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) for both NOFOs are available at https://www.nidcr.nih.gov/news-events/events/nidcr-preapplication-informational-webinar-community-based-participatory-research/faqs. The FAQs may be subsequently updated without notice. Prospective applicants are encouraged to visit this site often. The slides and a recording from the webinar will be available 7-10 business days after the webinar at https://www.nidcr.nih.gov/news-events/events/nidcr-preapplication-informational-webinar-community-based-participatory-research.
