Purpose

The purpose of this Notice is to inform potential applicants that the Eunice Kennedy Shriver National Institute of Child Health and Human Development (NICHD) will participate in PAR-24-204 “Summer Research Education Experience Program (R25 Clinical Trial Not Allowed),” effective for application due dates on or after May 8, 2026. The following changes and updates have been made to PAR-24-204 (shown in bold italics) to reflect NICHD's participation in this NOFO.
