Notice of Change: NIDCD Expiration of PAR-25-445, "NIDCD Clinical Research Center Grant (P50 Clinical Trial Optional)"
Notice Number:
NOT-DC-26-004

Key Dates

Release Date:
March 31, 2026

Related Announcements

  • September 15, 2025 - NIDCD Clinical Research Center Grant (P50 Clinical Trial Optional). See NOFO PAR-25-445.

Issued by

National Institute on Deafness and Other Communication Disorders (NIDCD)

Purpose

The purpose of this Notice is to inform the extramural community that PAR-25-445 “NIDCD Clinical Research Center Grant (P50 Clinical Trial Optional) will expire October 3, 2026. NIDCD plans to transition to a new option for supporting multi-disciplinary, collaborative team science. More details about this transition will be available in the future.

Key Dates

The NOFO currently reads:

Expiration Date: June 03, 2028

Revised to read: 

Expiration Date: October 03, 2026

There are no other changes to this NOFO.
