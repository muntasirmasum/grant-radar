Purpose

The National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases (NIDDK) will host a pre-application webinar to provide a program overview and respond to questions from prospective NIDDK applicants to PA-27-030 "Institutional Mentored Career Development Award (Parent K12) (Clinical Trials Not Allowed)". Prospective applicants are encouraged to visit the NIDDK Funding website to familiarize themselves with NIDDK's mission interests in Institutional Career Development.

Webinar Information

Date: Tuesday, September 15, 2026

Time: 3:00 - 4:00 PM Eastern Daylight Time (ET)

To join the webinar, follow the link below:

https://nih.zoomgov.com/j/1653217690?pwd=3QWLhVuxprd4H6umWvqjGhHxkcwJtY.1

Participation in the pre-application webinar, although encouraged, is optional and is not required for the submission of an application. Advanced registration is not required. We encourage potential applicants to send program-specific questions to the contacts listed under the "Inquiries" section of the Notice prior to the webinar.
