Purpose

The purpose of this Notice is to inform potential applicants of the changes in the Award Information for PAR-24-272 Clinical and Translational Science Award (UM1 Clinical Trial Optional) for receipt dates of May 28, 2026 and September 28, 2026. Changes to the table in the Award Budget section of the NOFO include specifying the applicable receipt dates (May 28, 2026 and September 28, 2026), an update under the heading “Maximum Direct Cost Budget Requests for UM1” to indicate amounts listed are based on FY2020-FY2024 NIH funding data and which amounts are applicable to each corresponding Hub Tier. All changes are indicated in bold italics under the “Revised to Read” section of this Notice.
