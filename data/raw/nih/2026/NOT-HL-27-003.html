Purpose

The purpose of this notice is to inform potential applicants of a change in the key dates for the Notice of Funding Opportunity (NOFO) PAR-25-032 "NHLBI Clinical Trial Pilot Studies (R34 Clinical Trial Optional)". Specifically, 1 additional receipt date has been added for new applications and resubmission/revision applications. The NOFO will now expire on March 17, 2027. 

The following sections of PAR-25-032 have been updated in bold italics to reflect the additional receipt dates and new expiration date.
