Purpose

The NIH SEED (Small business Education and Entrepreneurial Development) Office and the National Institute for General Medical Sciences (NIGMS) will hold a public pre-application webinar for the Notice of Funding Opportunity (NOFO) RFA-OD-27-009 Limited Competition: NIH Research Evaluation and Commercialization Hubs (REACH) (SB0 Clinical Trial Optional). 

The webinar will be on Wednesday, October 14 at 1:00 PM ET. SEED and  NIGMS staff will explain the goals and objectives of the REACH program and answer questions from attendees. Potential applicants are encouraged to submit their questions to [email protected] in advance of the webinar.

Note: Slides will be available at https://seed.nih.gov/portfolio/reach approximately 3 business days after the webinar.

Participation in these webinars, although encouraged, is optional and is not required for the submission of an application in response to RFA-OD-27-009.
