Purpose

The purpose of this Notice is to inform potential applicants of NCI's participation in PA-25-302, NIH Small Research Grant Program (Parent R03 Clinical Trial Not Allowed), effective for the June 16, 2026, due date and subsequent due dates.

The following changes and updates (shown in bold italics) are made to PA-25-302  to reflect NCI's participation in this notice of funding opportunity announcement (NOFO).
