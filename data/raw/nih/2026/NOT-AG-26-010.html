Purpose

The National Institute on Aging (NIA) will host a pre-application technical assistance webinar for individuals interested in applying for the Paul B. Beeson Emerging Leaders Career Development Award in Aging (K76 Clinical Trial Not Allowed) (PAR-27-112). The webinar will provide programmatic, peer review and award information, and guidance on the application process and grant writing strategies. Participation in the webinar, although encouraged, is not required for the submission of an application.

Below are key details regarding the webinar:

  • Title: Paul B. Beeson Emerging Leaders Career Development Award in Aging (K76) Technical Assistance Webinar

  • What: A live, pre-application technical assistance webinar regarding PAR-27-112

  • When: Monday, September 14, 2026

  • Where: Virtual; register here

  • Time: 2:00 pm – 3:00 pm Eastern Time

  • Are applicants required to participate: No
