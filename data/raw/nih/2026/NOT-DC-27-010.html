Purpose

This notice is to announce that the National Institute on Deafness and Other Communication Disorders (NIDCD) will host an informational webinar for individuals interested in NIDCD fellowship funding opportunities.

Event Date: Wednesday, October 28, 2026

Time: 12:00 PM – 1:30 PM ET

Virtual Meeting

During the information session, staff from the NIDCD and Center for Scientific Review will provide an overview of available individual fellowship opportunities, provide strategies for writing strong applications, and explain the peer review process. Prospective applicants are encouraged to attend and ask questions. An effort will be made to include ample time for questions.

The next individual fellowship application due date is December 8, 2026. Attending this information session is not a requirement for submission.

Why attend? Learn more about the NIDCD individual fellowship program, the application process, and tips for writing strong applications.

Who should attend? All individuals interested in applying for a fellowship award through NIDCD. Individuals should have research interests in hearing, balance, taste, smell, voice, speech, or language.
