Purpose

The purpose of this Notice is to inform potential applicants that the National Institute of Biomedical Imaging and Bioengineering is participating in PA-27-037, "Predoctoral to Postdoctoral Transition Award (Parent F99/K00 - Clinical Trial Not Allowed)" effective for receipt dates on or after December 08, 2026.   Part 1. Overview Information and Part 2. Section VII. Agency Contacts of the notice of funding opportunity have been updated accordingly.
