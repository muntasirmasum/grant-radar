Purpose

The purpose of this Notice is to inform potential applicants of NINDS's participation in PA-25-304: NIH Exploratory/Developmental Research Project Grant (Parent R21 Clinical Trial Not Allowed), effective for due dates on or after the June 16, 2026.

The following changes and updates (shown in bold italics) are made to PA-25-304 to reflect NINDS's participation in this Notice of Funding Opportunity (NOFO).
