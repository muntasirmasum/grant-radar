Purpose

The National Institute on Aging (NIA) will host a pre-application technical assistance webinar for individuals interested in applying for the NIA Expanding Research in AD/ADRD (ERA) R25 Postbaccalaureate and Summer Research Education Programs (RFA-AG-26-010 and RFA-AG-26-011). The webinar will provide programmatic, peer review and award information, and guidance on the application process and grant writing strategies. Participation in the webinar, although encouraged, is not required for the submission of an application.

Below are key details regarding the webinar:

  • Title: NIA ERA R25 Technical Assistance Webinar
  • What: A live, pre-application technical assistance webinar regarding RFA-AG-26-010 and RFA-AG-26-011
  • When: Wednesday, April 8, 2026
  • Where: Virtual; register here
  • Time: 1:00 pm – 2:00 pm Eastern Standard Time
  • Are applicants required to participate: No
