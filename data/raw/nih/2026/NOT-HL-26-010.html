Purpose

The purpose of this Notice is to update the award budget information in NHLBI's RFA-HL-27-004 "Limited Competition: Small Grant Program for NHLBI K01/K08/K23/K25 Recipients (R03 Clinical Trial Optional)". Specifically, direct costs are being reduced from $85,000 per year to $75,000 per year. This Notice applies to applications submitted to the October 6, 2026 due date and beyond.

Revised to Read

   Section II. Award Information

Award Budget:   

  • For applications submitted for the July 09, 2026 receipt date, application budgets are limited to direct costs up to $85,000 per year, but must reflect actual needs of the proposed project.
  • For receipt dates on or after October 06, 2026, application budgets are limited to direct costs up to $75,000 per year, but must reflect actual needs of the proposed project.
