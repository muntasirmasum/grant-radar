Purpose

The purpose of this notice is to inform the extramural community that effective May 08, 2026 NINDS will expire PA-25-150: Joint NINDS/NIMH Exploratory Neuroscience Research Grant (R21 Clinical Trial Not Allowed). No further applications will be accepted for this Notice of Funding Opportunity beyond the expiration date.
 

PA-25-150 - Key Dates

Currently Reads:

All applications are due by 5:00 PM local time of applicant organization.

Applicants are encouraged to apply early to allow adequate time to make any corrections to errors found in the application during the submission process by the due date.

Expiration Date

January 08, 2028
