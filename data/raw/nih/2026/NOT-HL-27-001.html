Purpose

The purpose of this Notice is to inform potential applicants that the National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute (NHLBI) will withdraw its participation, effective for due dates after July 5, 2026, in PA-25-305 "NIH Research Project Grant (Parent R01 Clinical Trial Required)".

The following sections of PA-25-305 (shown in bold and italics) have been updated to reflect the withdrawal of NHLBI's participation in this Notice of Funding Opportunity (NOFO).
