Purpose

This Notice is to inform applicants that the National Institute of Nursing Research (NINR) has joined the Notice of Funding Opportunity (NOFO) PAR-27-077, "NIH Science Education Partnership Award (SEPA) (R25 - Clinical Trial Not Allowed)," effective for application due dates on or after January 25, 2027.

The following sections of PAR-27-077 have been revised to reflect the participation of NINR in this NOFO (changes shown in bold italics):
