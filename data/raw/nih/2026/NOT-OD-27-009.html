Purpose

The National Institutes of Health (NIH) will host an informational webinar offering an overview of the Predoctoral to Postdoctoral Transition Award (PA-27-037) and answering questions from prospective applicants. Join staff from the Division of Biomedical Research Workforce (DBRW) and NIH Institutes and Centers (IC) for this live technical assistance session on Monday, November 2, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. ET. The webinar will cover programmatic and peer review considerations, the application process, and grant-writing strategies.
