Purpose

The purpose of this Notice is to extend the expiration date for PAR-25-095, "Secondary Analysis and Integration of Existing Data to Elucidate Cancer Risk and Related Outcomes (R01 Clinical Trial Not Allowed)".

Therefore, with the addition/extension by one Application Due Date, PAR-25-095 now expires on November 6, 2026.

The following sections in Part 1. Overview Information, Key Dates section have been changed:  

Currently reads and modified to read (in bold italics):

Currently reads:

Expiration Date: September 08, 2026

Modified to read (in italics):

Expiration Date: November 6, 2026

All other aspects of PAR-25-095 remain unchanged.
