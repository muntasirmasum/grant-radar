Purpose

The National Institute of General Medical Sciences (NIGMS) will host an informational webinar to:

  • Provide a broad overview of the training, fellowship, research education and career development funding opportunities we support.
  • Have breakout sessions to answer questions about our interests in newly published Parent R25 and K12 Notices of funding opportunities. 

We encourage you to review the NIGMS ICO profile page for more information on our training, fellowship, research education, and career development (institutional and individual) priorities.
