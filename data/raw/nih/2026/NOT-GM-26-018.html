Purpose

The National Institute of General Medical Sciences (NIGMS) will host an informational webinar to provide a program overview and respond to questions from prospective applicants to the Instrumentation Program for Resource-Limited Institutions (RLI-S10) (PAR-27-067). The next application deadline is January 26, 2027.

The RLI-S10 program: 

  • Provides funding for purchase of modern scientific instruments at resource-limited institutions 

  • Expands education and research capacity in basic, translational, and biomedically related behavioral or clinical fields
