Purpose

The National Cancer Institute (NCI) will hold public pre-application webinars for each NCI Community Oncology Research Program (NCORP) RFA. 

The public pre-application webinar for the Notice of Funding Opportunity (NOFO) RFA-CA-27-006 entitled "NCI Community Oncology Research Program (NCORP) Research Bases (UG1 Clinical Trial Required)". 

The webinar will be on Wednesday, June 17 at 3:30-5:00pm (ET). NCI staff members involved in this NOFO will provide orientation and technical assistance to potential applicants by explaining the goals and objectives of the NCORP Research Bases and answering questions from attendees about the NCORP Program.
