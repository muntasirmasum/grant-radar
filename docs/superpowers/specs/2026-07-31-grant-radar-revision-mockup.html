Details worth confirming

  • Brand mark: "Grant Radar" in Newsreader with a small purple radar-ping dot. It reads as a sibling of your site without pretending to be a page of it; the navbar links back to muntasirmasum.com.
  • Three content states: enriched cards get a purple-edged TL;DR block (+ do/don't when expanded); un-enriched FOAs show the Grants.gov Synopsis in quieter grey — honest, but never an empty "pending" apology; bare notices stay compact with no body text at all.
  • Urgency encoding: due dates within ~30 days get the solid purple chip; further out, gold-tint. Policy/admin items get gold left-edges and never show deadline chips.
  • "For you" line: match reasons in words on the card, no percentage scores anywhere.
